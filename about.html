About Me

Designing tomorrow's digital experiences, today

My Story

I came to UX design because I got tired of watching people struggle with systems that weren't built for them. Working in social services, caregiving, and case management, I saw firsthand how poorly designed systems created unnecessary barriers - preventing people from getting the help they needed, not because resources weren't available, but because the experience was broken. I realized I could help one person navigate a flawed system, or I could redesign the system to work for everyone. I chose design because I want to create permanent, meaningful change at scale.

My background in biopsychology, cognition, and neuroscience taught me how people think and process information. My years in direct service taught me how to truly listen - not just to what people say, but to what they need. I approach design challenges with the mindset of a researcher and the heart of an advocate. I believe the best solutions emerge when we dig deep into user research, uncover the real problem, and stay committed to solving it in ways that remove friction from people's everyday lives.

Whether I'm redesigning an app's retention flow or creating navigation systems for commuters, I combine analytical rigor with genuine empathy. For me, design isn't just about aesthetics - it's about fundamentally shifting how organizations structure experiences so that people's lives actually get easier.

My Core Values

These principles guide every decision I make as a designer and shape how I approach challenges.

Always stay learning

Design evolves constantly. I commit to continuous growth, staying curious, and embracing new tools, techniques, and perspectives to create better experiences.

Navigate every day with intention

Every design choice should have purpose. I believe in thoughtful, deliberate decisions that serve users and align with clear goals.

Design with depth, not just decoration

Beautiful interfaces matter, but true design goes deeper. I focus on solving real problems, understanding context, and creating experiences that genuinely improve people's lives.
