01 — OVERVIEW

The Challenge

Urban commuters were juggling multiple apps to plan their trips - checking transit apps for trains, Google Maps for traffic, bike-share apps, and payment systems for fares. NextStop consolidates all transportation options into one organized view with a unique traffic radar feature.
